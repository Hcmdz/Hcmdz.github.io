Privnum

v1.1.0 · GPL-3.0
Android Kotlin MinSDK 29 TargetSDK 36

Private on-device caller ID for Android: unknown callers identified from a local database that never leaves your phone.

Why It Exists

Unknown numbers keep calling while your contacts live on someone else’s server. Privnum keeps its own private directory right on your phone — organized, searchable, backed up as VCF — so you always know who is calling, with zero network permissions.

Private Caller ID

Incoming and outgoing call popup with name, photo and notes resolved from the on-device database.

Multiple Numbers

Several numbers per contact with a primary flag, per-number actions and duplicate protection.

VCF Backup

Import, export and share contacts with photos included — nothing ever leaves the device.

FTS Search

Instant prefix search across names, notes and every digit of every stored number.

Lockdown

Optional passcode with 5-attempt lockout, biometric unlock and auto-lock timeouts.

Material You

Dynamic color with toggle, pure-black AMOLED mode, and light, dark and system themes.

Contact Photos

Gallery or camera pictures, cropped square on-device, shown in list, details and call popup.

Security

Zero network permissions, encrypted storage, backup disabled, release log stripping.

Offline by Design

No accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting — the database never leaves your phone.
