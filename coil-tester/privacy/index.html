Summary

CoilTester is designed to work entirely on your device. The developer collects no analytics, runs no ads, and operates no server. Your data stays on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

CoilTester stores the following data locally in app-private storage on your device:

  • Settings — sensitivity, language, theme, thresholds, and calibration data
  • Calibration data — stored offset/scale values, resettable from Settings

Sensor readings (magnetometer) are processed in memory and never persisted beyond the current session. None of this data is sent to the developer or any external server.

Permissions

CoilTester requests the following permissions:

  • BODY_SENSORS / HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS — magnetometer access
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback on detection
  • WAKE_LOCK — keep screen awake during detection
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SENSOR — foreground sensor service

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot make network requests. It does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, or any other sensitive data.

Third-Party Services

No third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking services are integrated in CoilTester. Code libraries bundled with the app are listed in THIRD_PARTY.md. The developer operates no server.

Security

  • All data is stored locally in your device's app-private storage
  • No data is transmitted to the developer's servers (the developer operates no servers)
  • Network security configuration blocks cleartext (HTTP) traffic to every host
  • allowBackup is set to false, so app data cannot be extracted via ADB backup
  • FLAG_SECURE is enabled on the detection screen to prevent screenshots and screen recording
  • Release builds are minified and strip all log calls

No Analytics or Tracking

CoilTester does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDKs. The developer does not receive information about how you use the app.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access — all your data is visible within the app
  • Deletion — reset calibration from Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all data

Since everything is stored locally on your device, the developer has no access to your data and cannot delete it on your behalf — you manage it directly through the app or by uninstalling.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not operate any service that collects personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
