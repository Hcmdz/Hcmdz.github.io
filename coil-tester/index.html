CoilTester

v1.0.0 · GPL-3.0 Stable
Android Kotlin MinSDK 24 TargetSDK 35

Turn your phone's magnetometer into a calibrated AC/DC coil-testing instrument.

Why It Exists

Testing solenoid coils in the field usually means carrying dedicated equipment. CoilTester puts a calibrated AC/DC magnetic-field detector in your pocket — real-time readings, instant haptic and audio feedback, and a guided distance target — so you can verify coil operation anywhere, fully offline.

Haptic & Audio

Instant feedback on detection, with adjustable volume and vibration.

Calibration

Stored offset/scale data with a one-tap reset option.

Distance Guide

10 cm target to position the sensor over the coil.

Advanced Options

Custom AC/DC thresholds and mains frequency band (50 / 60 Hz).

Material You

Dynamic color, edge-to-edge layout, light / dark / pure-black (AMOLED) themes.

Multilingual

English, French, and Arabic localization with full RTL support.

Security

FLAG_SECURE, cleartext blocked, app backups disabled, ProGuard log stripping.

Offline-first

No INTERNET permission, no accounts, no tracking.
