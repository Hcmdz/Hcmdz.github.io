ElecPilot

Android Kotlin MinSDK 29 TargetSDK 36

Professional Android app for managing electrical motor starters and PLC I/O modules in industrial environments.

Why It Exists

Keeping track of motor starters and PLC I/O modules across an installation is tedious on paper and easy to get wrong. ElecPilot puts the whole picture on your phone — organized, searchable, and safely backed up — so engineers spend less time on record-keeping and more time on the job.

Motor Starters

Full CRUD, search, batch edit, and statistics for motor starter records.

PLC I/O Modules

Dedicated database and views for PLC I/O modules, kept separate and clean.

Cloud Backup

Google Drive & OneDrive via rclone, with AES-256-GCM encrypted config.

Excel/CSV

Import/export, blank template generation, and scheduled local backups.

Material You

Dynamic color, edge-to-edge layout, and light & dark themes.

Multilingual

English, French, and Arabic localization with full RTL support.

Voice Search

Speak to filter motor starters hands-free.

Security

FLAG_SECURE, encrypted storage, WebView allowlist, ProGuard log stripping.
